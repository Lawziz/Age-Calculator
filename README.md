# Age Calculator

A sleek, artistic browser app that transforms your birthday into a radiant life timeline. Enter your date of birth and watch the app reveal your age in years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes, and seconds — with a joyful countdown to your next birthday.

## Features

- Elegant date-based age calculator
- Displays current age in multiple units of time
- Shows your formatted date of birth and weekday
- Includes a countdown to your next birthday
- Birthday celebration animation with confetti

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Select your date of birth.
3. Click the submit button.
4. See your age and next birthday countdown instantly.

## Tech

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- `canvas-confetti` for birthday celebration effects

## Notes

- The date picker prevents future birth dates.
- Designed for a simple, polished online experience.
